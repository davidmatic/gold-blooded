# Gold Blooded

An unofficial, non-commercial San Francisco 49ers fan site. It publishes weekly analysis, news, and an unabashedly adoring section devoted to Brock Purdy.

## Calendar

**Game Week**:
The cycle from the day after one 49ers game through the end of the next one. It is the unit the site's game content is organised around. Each playoff game is its own Game Week.
_Avoid_: week, matchup week

**Bye Week**:
A Game Week with no game. It carries news and a season recap but no Game Preview.

**Offseason**:
The period between the 49ers' final game of a season and the next season's games. Game content pauses; the focus shifts to the draft, free agency, and the Purdy Shrine.

## Content

**Game Week Page**:
The page for one Game Week. It brings together the Game Preview, the Stat Breakdown, and the Highlights from the previous game.

**Game Preview**:
The narrative take on the upcoming game: the opponent, storylines, keys to the game, and a prediction.
_Avoid_: matchup preview, preview

**Stat Breakdown**:
The numerical analysis of the upcoming game: team and player stats against this opponent, plus trends.
_Avoid_: stats section, statistical analysis

**Season Stats**:
Team and player statistics for the whole season up to the latest game.
_Avoid_: season-to-date, cumulative stats

**Highlights**:
Written spotlights on the standout players from the previous game, with official video embedded where it exists.
_Avoid_: recap, player highlights

**News Summary**:
A short write-up, in the site's own words, of one piece of recent team news. It always links to its original source. Its category is one of the following: Injury, Personnel, Practice Report, or Coach's Comments.
_Avoid_: article, news item

**Purdy Shrine**:
The section devoted to Brock Purdy, written as if we are hopelessly in love with him. It holds the weekly Love Letter, his stat tracker, the running list of reasons we love him, and his origin story.
_Avoid_: Purdy page, Purdy section

**Film Room**:
The section with credited, linked summaries of other analysts' film breakdowns, such as The QB School and Jonnydel's Football Academy. Its Purdy-related entries also appear in the Purdy Shrine.
_Avoid_: analysis, breakdowns

**Love Letter**:
The Purdy Shrine's weekly piece, written after each game. It stays adoring no matter how the game went.

**Prediction Record**:
The running tally of how each Game Preview's prediction (winner and score) compared with the actual result.
_Avoid_: pick record, scorecard

**Free Agency Tracker**:
The Offseason list of 49ers signings, departures, and re-signings.

**Draft Coverage**:
The Offseason content about the NFL Draft: team needs, a mock draft roundup, the draft-day pick tracker, and profiles of the players drafted.

**Archive**:
The collection of past Game Week Pages, kept so readers can look back.

## Sources

**Preferred Source**:
A source the content draws on first: the official NFL injury report, 49ers.com, NBC Sports Bay Area, ESPN, the San Francisco Chronicle, nflverse, the 49ers Talk and Jonnydel's Football Academy podcasts, and The QB School. Other sources are allowed, as long as they are cited.
_Avoid_: approved source, whitelist
