On Jonnydel's Football Academy, Jonnydel and his co-host react to the 49ers' win over the Vikings in Mexico City. They start with the injury to right tackle Colton McKivitz, and Jonnydel argues the Vikings' reaction showed the brotherhood in the league. He also pushes back on the idea that the injury came from a dirty play.

The main focus is Brock Purdy and Kyle Shanahan against the top-ranked Vikings defense. Jonnydel says Brian Flores leaned on simulated pressures and that Purdy beat them, including a throw to George Kittle that he calls a man-beater against Harrison Smith. He singles out a seven-step drop from the 49ers' own five-yard line as a sign of how much Shanahan trusts Purdy. The show cites Purdy's 146 passer rating against Minnesota, well above any other quarterback. They also credit Mike Evans as the true X receiver Jonnydel has long wanted for this offense.

The show then turns to the defense: pressure on Kirk Cousins, the pick-six, Marques Sigle's play at safety, Renardo Green's coverage, and the roster's limited injured-reserve return spots.
