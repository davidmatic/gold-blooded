On Jonnydel's Football Academy, Jonnydel and his co-host preview the 49ers' trip to face the Vikings in Mexico City. They open with the league's handling of Puka Nacua's suspension, which Jonnydel calls unfair, and then move to the playoff picture and the tough stretch of games ahead.

Jonnydel argues the idea that Brian Flores has Kyle Shanahan's number is mostly a narrative. In his view, Minnesota has simply been a very good defense that puts pressure on interior offensive linemen with a mix of disguised coverages. He says Brock Purdy's strength is processing what he sees, but heavy chip-and-release protection limits which concepts the offense can run. His answer is a strong run game that keeps the 49ers out of long third downs.

They also cover Keenan Allen's growing route tree, the matchup between Jaelon Jennings and Upton Stout, whether to activate Nate Hobbs, and Jonnydel's concerns about the safeties and about designed quarterback runs for Purdy. Jonnydel ends with a prediction of a low-scoring 49ers win.
