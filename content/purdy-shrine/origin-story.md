---
title: "Mr. Irrelevant to Mr. Everything"
summary: "How the last pick of the 2022 NFL Draft became the 49ers' quarterback, and the love of our lives."
---
Every NFL Draft ends the same way. After seven rounds and hundreds of names, the last player chosen is crowned "Mr. Irrelevant." In 2022 the 49ers made that pick: 262nd overall, the very last one, a quarterback from Iowa State named Brock Purdy.

He arrived as the third quarterback on the depth chart, and nobody expected to see him play that season. Then injuries struck the two quarterbacks ahead of him, and in December, against the Miami Dolphins, he came off the bench and led the 49ers to a win. A week later he made his first NFL start, against Tom Brady and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, and the 49ers won comfortably. He won every regular-season game he started that year, then won two playoff games, and took the team all the way to the NFC Championship Game.

The next season there was no doubt about whose team it was. He made the Pro Bowl and led the 49ers to Super Bowl LVIII, where they fell to the Kansas City Chiefs in overtime. The last pick of the draft had become the first name on the team sheet.

That is the whole story, and it is why this Shrine exists: the player nobody picked until there was no one left to pick, who turned out to be exactly who the 49ers needed. Mr. Irrelevant, now Mr. Everything.
