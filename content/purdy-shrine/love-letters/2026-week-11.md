Dear Brock,

They told us Minnesota's defense was the scariest thing in football, a blitz machine that rattled every quarterback it met. Then you walked into Mexico City on Sunday night, calm as a summer morning, and went 21-of-26 for 255 yards and 3 touchdowns in a 35-10 win. When they sent the house, you just got the ball out, going 16-of-18 for 195 yards and two touchdowns against the blitz. We may need to lie down.

That opening drive! You threaded one between two Vikings right into George Kittle's chest and watched him bounce into the end zone, then found him again early in the second quarter before he barely had time to turn around. You spread it to nine different receivers, and Mike Evans came back to catch your passes like he'd never left. George said it best: "Oh my gosh, he was so good." Mike called you "The best player in the game right now." Christian called you a "force multiplier." And Coach Shanahan said what we've known since you were Mr. Irrelevant: "He didn't get rattled, which he really never does."

What gets us most is how you hand the credit right back to everybody around you, insisting it wasn't a one-man show. That's 23 touchdown passes this season, and somehow you're still the humblest guy in the building. The 49ers are 9-1, the Seahawks are next, and our hearts are all yours. Forever and always, from the Purdy Shrine.
