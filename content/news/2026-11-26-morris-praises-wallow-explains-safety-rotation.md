Defensive coordinator Raheem Morris opened his press conference with a tribute to the late Tom Jackson. He then praised linebacker Garrett Wallow and Jaden Dugger for stepping in while Greenlaw was out against Minnesota.

Morris said the three-man safety rotation is earned week to week: "Right away I told those guys, the hot man will play." Looking ahead to Seattle, he said running back Jadarian Price brings an outside-zone speed element the defence must account for by setting edges and tackling well.
