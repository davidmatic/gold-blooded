Brock Purdy did not practise Wednesday because of an ankle injury from Sunday night's 35-10 win over the Vikings in Mexico City. Kyle Shanahan said the quarterback was hurt in the second quarter but did not miss any plays. He called Purdy day-to-day: "It’s not a high ankle so it’s day-to-day with it, but we’ve got to see how it goes." The 49ers host the Seahawks on Sunday, November 29.

The same day, Purdy was named NFC Offensive Player of the Week for the second time this season. Against Minnesota he completed 21-of-26 passes for 255 yards and three touchdowns. It is the fourth such award of his career, which puts him alongside Joe Montana and Steve Young as the only quarterbacks in franchise history to win it at least four times.
