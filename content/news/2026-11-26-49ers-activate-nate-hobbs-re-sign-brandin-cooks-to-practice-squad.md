The 49ers returned CB Nate Hobbs to the active roster on November 25 and signed WR Brandin Cooks to the practice squad. To make room, they moved OL Colton McKivitz to a reserve list. Hobbs signed with San Francisco in March and has appeared in 62 career games with 43 starts. Cooks appeared in five games for the 49ers this season before being waived on November 21.

McKivitz started 10 games this season. Shanahan confirmed the team will not have him the rest of the way and praised his play: "I thought he was playing really well." Asked whether Vederian Lowe will be the starting right tackle going forward, Shanahan answered, "Yes."
