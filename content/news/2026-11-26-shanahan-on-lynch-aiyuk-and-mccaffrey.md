Asked about general manager John Lynch's contract extension, Kyle Shanahan said he expects the two to finish together: "I’ve always seen myself being here only with John and I think he feels the same."

On Brandon Aiyuk, who the team has parted ways with and who has reportedly joined the Washington Commanders, Shanahan said: "None. It was just a transactional thing and it’s good for Washington."

Shanahan credited Christian McCaffrey's preparation for his longevity: "That’s why we call him a psycho because it’s not normal." He said McCaffrey understands the benefits of sharing work with Kaelon Black. He also confirmed that the "unc" nickname he uses applies only to Mike Evans and Keenan Allen.
