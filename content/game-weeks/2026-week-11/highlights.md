That was the most complete game of the season. Against a Minnesota defense that came in allowing the fewest points in the league and blitzing on almost every snap, the 49ers went 35-10 at Estadio Banorte. They scored on the opening drive, kept the pressure coming, and put it away just before halftime when Ji'Ayir Brown took a pick to the house. The Faithful turned Mexico City into a home game, Kyle Shanahan got his 100th career win, and San Francisco moves to 9-1.

The story was Brock Purdy beating the blitz. He went 16-of-18 for 195 yards and two touchdowns when Minnesota sent extra rushers, and he spread the ball to nine different receivers. The Niners became the first team this season to score more than 23 points on this Vikings defense. Next up is Seattle at Levi's.
